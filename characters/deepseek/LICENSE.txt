They are fan art of the DeepSeek whale girl ("DeepSeek娘", "藍色大肥魚"), a community
character that is not an official DeepSeek design:
- the original character "溟月" by 上善无形 (June 2025), who opened her for derivative
  works under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 on 2 August 2026;
- the maid whale girl design by ZipZipPipe, built on 溟月.

The images were drawn with ChatGPT image generation from that design and edited for
Mom Pet (cut out, resized, colours of one hand fixed). You may not use these images
commercially, and anything you make from them must be shared under the same license.
